### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This document defines the complete narrative and conversion copy for the **Entrelinhas Store** landing page.

In strict compliance with **MANIFESTO.md**, every sentence is grounded in **confirmed business facts**: 26 years in business (*desde 1998*), 5.0 Google rating, physical store in Jardim Calux (SBC), tailoring & casual collection (*alfaiataria e casual*), custom uniforms, and direct WhatsApp sales with nationwide delivery.

All generic AI writing tropes (*"transform your experience"*, *"where passion meets detail"*, *"elevate your business"*) have been eliminated in favor of human, specific, neighborhood-rooted Brazilian Portuguese.

### 1. CONVERSION STRATEGY & ARCHETYPE

* **Primary Conversion Goal:** Initiate a direct WhatsApp consultation or order (`+55 11 91480-2056`).
* **Primary Call-to-Action (CTA):** "Falar no WhatsApp com a Entrelinhas"
* **Secondary Actions:**
  1. "Como Chegar na Loja (Jardim Calux)"
  2. "Pedir Orçamento de Uniformes"
* **Main Customer Objection Handled:** Frustration with shopping mall crowds, high prices, impersonal service, and garments that only fit standard model sizes.
* **Core Conversion Narrative:** Position Entrelinhas Store as the warm, neighborhood boutique with 26 years of tailoring craftsmanship where customers get high-quality clothes for all body shapes (*para todos os manequins*) at fair prices, with direct care from the owners.
